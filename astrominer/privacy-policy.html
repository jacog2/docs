Astrominer Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

Astrominer is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what information the app uses and how optional advertising and local reminder features work.

Information The App Uses

Astrominer stores local game progress on the device, including resources, upgrades, settings, daily streaks, survey expedition timers, offline-haul timing, achievements, and progression state.

Android backup and device transfer may preserve local game progress through the device owner's Google or Android backup settings. Astrominer's app-defined backup rules include only the local game-save file.

The app does not currently include player accounts, purchases, chat, leaderboards, cloud save, or user-generated content.

Advertising And Consent

Astrominer uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may be used for a temporary mining boost, a daily bounty refresh, or an offline-haul double. Watching ads is optional and is not required to play.

Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, and usage information to provide, measure, and secure ads. Astrominer uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to expose privacy choices when available.

Daily Reminders

Daily Reminders are optional local notifications. If enabled, Astrominer schedules a local notification on the device to remind players about supply drops and idle haul. Reminder settings are stored locally and are not sent to a developer server. On Android versions that require notification permission, Astrominer asks for that permission only when reminders are enabled.

Data Sharing

Astrominer does not sell player data. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google Mobile Ads and its partners to provide, measure, and secure ads. No Astrominer account data is shared because the app does not currently include accounts.

Data Retention

Local save data remains on the device until the player clears app data or uninstalls the app, subject to Android backup and device-transfer behavior controlled by the device owner. Advertising data retention is governed by Google and the configured AdMob settings.

Contact

For privacy questions about Astrominer, use the public project contact page at https://github.com/jacog2/docs/issues.
